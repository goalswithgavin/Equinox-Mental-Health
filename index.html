Photo of Lucia Savage-Reeder

Twenty years of steady, human care.

Lucia opened Equinox Mental Health in 2026 after two decades of counseling work in residential, community-based, and outpatient settings. She works with individuals age fourteen and up, meeting each person where they are and helping them move, at their own pace, toward the change they're already reaching for.

Her approach is client-centered and grounded in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy — practical, collaborative, and paced to fit the person in front of her rather than a script.

M.A., Counseling Psychology — Antioch University New England, 2006 B.A., Psychology — Colby-Sawyer College, 2002 Licensed Clinical Mental Health Counselor, NH Master Licensed Alcohol & Drug Counselor, NH

Areas of focus

Every person arrives with a different weight to set down. These are the places Lucia most often works alongside her clients.

  • Anxiety & depression

    Understanding the patterns that keep a mind on edge or worn down, and building steadier ground underneath them.

  • Grief

    Space to move through loss at your own pace, without a timeline imposed from outside.

  • Relationship issues

    Working through conflict, communication, and connection — with partners, family, or within yourself.

  • Substance use & addiction

    Compassionate, non-judgmental support for substance use and recovery, from someone who has spent a career in this work.

  • Co-occurring disorders

    Integrated care when mental health and substance use are intertwined, rather than treated as separate problems.

How sessions work

Therapy here starts with listening. Using a client-centered Cognitive Behavioral approach, Lucia helps clients notice the thoughts and patterns driving a difficult moment, and build practical, workable ways through it — at a pace that respects where you are, not where a treatment plan says you should be.

Lucia has worked with adolescents and adults across residential, community-based, and office-based outpatient settings, and welcomes clients age fourteen and up. Whatever brought you here, the first conversation is simply that — a conversation.
